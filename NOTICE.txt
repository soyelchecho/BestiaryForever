Data credits
------------
BestiaryForever/Data/Loot.lua is generated by tools/build_bestiary_data.py from data published by
ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net). Data from ForeverDB.net.
ForeverDB's addon data is GPL-3.0 with an attribution term (GPL-3.0 section 7(b)): anyone who reuses
or redistributes this data must keep this credit, with the link to https://foreverdb.net, wherever
the data is shown.

BestiaryForever/Data/Loot.lua also includes zone names, zone outlines (used to place creatures in
zones), dungeon names and, in BestiaryForever/Data/Items.lua, English and Spanish item names, all
from ForeverDB.net. Data from ForeverDB.net.

Creature levels, rank, type, model and spawn points come from CMaNGOS classic-db
(https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db, GPL-3.0). Part of the drop rates are Classic data that ForeverDB
also takes from CMaNGOS classic-db, labelled "Classic*" in the addon. Classic data may differ in WoW Forever.
